# The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the File They Cannot Convert

One satirical sentence, entered in the make.fun bounty
[u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7](https://make.fun/competitions/u-swift-raven-16-the-long-roast-lawmakers-and-the-f3a3bdf7).

> The senator writing the law that tells artificial intelligence what it is allowed to think just emailed his intern the bill as a .docx and asked her to turn it into a PDF for him.

`main.py` prints the sentence to stdout and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.
Deterministic: no randomness, no network, no input.
